Buy and Sell Stocks 3
Difficulty: HARD | Max Score: 75
You are given an array prices[], where prices[i] represents the price of a stock on the i-th day. Your task is to determine the maximum profit you can achieve by performing at most two transactions.



A transaction consists of:

Buying one stock, and
Selling that stock later. 


Important Rules

You may complete at most two transactions.
You cannot hold more than one stock at a time (i.e., you must sell the stock before buying again).


Input Format

The first line contains an integer n, the number of days.
The second line contains n space-separated integers representing the array prices[].


Output Format

Print a single integer — the maximum profit achievable.


Constraints:

1 ≤ n ≤ 105
0 ≤ prices[i] ≤ 105


Example 1:

Input: 
8
3 3 5 0 0 3 1 4

Output: 6

Explanation: Buy on day 4 (price = 0), sell on day 6 (price = 3), and profit = 3 - 0 = 3.
Then buy on day 7 (price = 1) and sell on day 8 (price = 4), profit = 4 - 1 = 3.


Example 2:

Input: 
5
1 2 3 4 5

Output: 4

Explanation: Buy on day 1 (price = 1), sell on day 5 (price = 5), and profit = 5 - 1 = 4.
Note that you cannot buy on day 1, buy on day 2, and sell them later, as you are engaging in multiple transactions simultaneously. You must sell before buying again.
